Welcome to FinLearn

Learn Before You Choose

FinLearn is an educational portal designed to help people understand everyday banking and personal finance. Learn about savings accounts, cards, loans, interest rates, EMIs and responsible money management.

Good financial planning can help you save regularly, manage expenses and make informed borrowing decisions.

Remember: understanding the terms and charges of a financial product is important before applying for it.

Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account allows individuals to deposit money, withdraw funds and earn interest on eligible balances. It is commonly used for everyday savings and emergency funds.

Before opening an account, check the minimum balance, service charges, interest rate and available digital services.

Current Account

Current accounts are generally designed for frequent transactions and business-related banking requirements. They may have different fees and balance requirements compared with savings accounts.

Understanding Cards

Debit Card

A debit card allows you to make payments using money available in your linked bank account.

  • Useful for everyday purchases
  • Can be used at ATMs
  • Money is generally deducted from the bank account
  • Can support online and contactless payments

Credit Card

A credit card provides access to a predefined credit limit. If the outstanding amount is not paid according to the card's terms, interest and other charges may apply.

Tip: Pay your credit-card bill on time and avoid borrowing more than you can comfortably repay.

Understanding Loans

Personal Loan

A personal loan provides borrowed funds that are generally repaid through scheduled installments. The interest rate, processing fee and repayment period depend on the lender and applicant's circumstances.

Education Loan

Education loans can help eligible students finance approved education expenses. Applicants should understand repayment conditions, interest rates and applicable eligibility requirements.

Home Loan

A home loan is typically used to finance the purchase, construction or improvement of a residential property. Borrowers usually repay the loan over a longer period.

Borrow Responsibly

Compare the total repayment cost rather than looking only at the advertised interest rate. Read all terms, fees and conditions before accepting a loan.

Important Financial Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage charged on borrowed money or paid on eligible deposits over a specified period.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment, a regular payment made toward repaying a loan.
Principal
The original amount of money borrowed or invested, excluding interest.
Tenure
The length of time over which a financial product or loan remains active or is repaid.

Steps to Open a Bank Account

  1. Choose the type of account you need.
  2. Compare account features, charges and requirements.
  3. Check the eligibility criteria.
  4. Complete the application form.
  5. Submit the required identification and address documents.
  6. Complete the required verification process.
  7. Make any required initial deposit.
  8. Receive your account details and banking access.

Responsible Personal Finance Tips

  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Track your income and expenses.
  • Build an emergency fund.
  • Pay bills and loan EMIs on time.
  • Avoid unnecessary high-interest debt.
  • Compare fees before choosing financial products.
  • Protect your banking passwords and OTPs.
  • Review your financial goals regularly.
  • Save a portion of your income consistently.
  • Read all financial documents before signing.
"An informed financial decision today can create greater financial confidence tomorrow."

Never share your OTP, PIN or password with anyone claiming to be a bank representative.
