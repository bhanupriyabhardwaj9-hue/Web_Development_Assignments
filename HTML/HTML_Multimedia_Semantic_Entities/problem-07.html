Project Overview

Welcome to Skyline Heights

Skyline Heights is a modern residential project designed for comfortable and convenient urban living. The project offers spacious apartments, landscaped gardens, security and excellent connectivity.

Choose from carefully planned 2 BHK, 3 BHK and 4 BHK apartments with contemporary interiors and premium facilities. Homes start from ₹65 lakh.

Early buyers can receive a special booking offer. The offer is valid until 30 September 31 October 2026.

Project specifications follow applicable building standards and provide approximately 24-hour security and essential resident services.
