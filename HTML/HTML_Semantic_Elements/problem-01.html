Welcome to GLA University

GLA University is a leading educational institution offering undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering, management, computer applications and other disciplines.

Our university focuses on academic excellence, practical learning, innovation and overall student development. Students get opportunities to participate in projects, competitions, internships and extracurricular activities.

Academic Programs

B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering

The B.Tech CSE program provides knowledge of programming, data structures, databases, computer networks, artificial intelligence and software development.

Duration: 4 Years

B.Tech Artificial Intelligence & Data Science

This program focuses on artificial intelligence, machine learning, statistics, data analysis and modern computing technologies.

Duration: 4 Years

BBA – Bachelor of Business Administration

BBA develops business, leadership, communication and management skills required for modern organizations.

Duration: 3 Years

Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility requirements vary according to the selected program. Applicants should carefully check the academic requirements before submitting an application.

  2. Enter personal and academic information.

  7. Track the application and admission status.
