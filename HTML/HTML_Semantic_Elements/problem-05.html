Event Overview

Welcome to the National Career & Education Fair. This premier event is organized to bridge the gap between talented students, active job seekers, leading corporate organizations, and renowned educational institutions.

Objectives

Our main goal is to provide a single interactive platform for career development, networking, and higher education exploration.

Participant Guidance

"Your career is a journey, not a destination. Take this opportunity to network, ask questions, and explore diverse pathways." — Organizing Committee

Preparation Guide

Quick Tip: Print at least 15 copies of your updated resume and dress in professional business attire!

Preparation Checklist (Unordered List)

  • Research the participating companies beforehand.
  • Prepare a 30-second elevator pitch introducing yourself.
  • Keep your digital portfolio links ready via QR codes.

Attendee Journey (Ordered List)

  1. Complete online portal registration.
  2. Receive confirmation ID via email.
  3. Check-in at the venue entrance badge counter.
  4. Attend the targeted industry speaker tracks.
  5. Visit designated company booths for walk-in interviews.
