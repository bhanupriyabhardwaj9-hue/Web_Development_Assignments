Employee Leave Request

Employees can use this form to submit a leave request. Please provide accurate employee details, select the type of leave, specify the dates, and give a brief reason for your absence.

Leave Categories

Leave Category Description
Casual Leave For personal or short-term requirements.
Sick Leave For illness, medical treatment or recovery.
Earned Leave Planned leave earned by the employee.
Work From Home Allows the employee to work remotely.
Other For leave requests that do not fit the above categories.
