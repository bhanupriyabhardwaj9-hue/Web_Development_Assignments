GLA University

Admission Overview

GLA University, Greater Noida, admission 2026 is open. The university offers entrance-based for its UG and PG courses. Admission to all courses is based on performance in the GLAET entrance test conducted by the university. Selection is based on performance of entrance exam followed by personal interview. GLA University, Greater Noida offers an online as well as offline mode of application. The university offer courses such as: BTech, BBA, MBA and MCA. It offer courses in affiliation with GLA University. Candidates looking for admission to any course are required to visit the official website of the university. To know more about GLA University – Greater Noida admission 2026, go through the following section:

Eligibility

GLA University, Greater Noida admission requires valid score in GLAET, followed by personal interview. The selection criteria for all the courses is entrance-based. For admission into UG and PG courses, the common eligibility criteria is as below:
UG courses (BBA and BTech): Pass Class 12 with at least 50% aggregate score
UG courses (BBA and BTech): Pass Class 12 with at least 50% aggregate score

Application process

GLA University Application Process 2026
GLA University, Greater Noida admission process 2026 starts with the application process. Candidates can download the application process from the university's official website. The duly filled application form has to be sent either through post or submitted in person at the concerned department at GLA University. However, the application and entrance test fee will be paid online, and candidates have to attach the pay slip along with two copies of the online bank challan with the application form.
Selection Rounds
Admission to all the PG courses at GLA University, Greater Noida, is entrance-based. Candidates have to qualify for university-level entrance exam called GLAET and personal interview.
Merit List Release & Counselling
After the completion of all the selection rounds, candidates have to appear for the counselling process for the respective courses. Later on, the university will release the merit list in various rounds for the selected candidates. Students who find their names on the merit list need to pay a certain amount of the GLA University, Greater Noida fee to secure their seats.
