Spice Garden Restaurant

Welcome to Spice Garden Restaurant. We serve delicious Indian dishes prepared with fresh ingredients and traditional spices. Our menu includes tasty starters, main courses, desserts and refreshing beverages for everyone.

Starters

  • Paneer Tikka - Grilled cottage cheese with onions and capsicum.
  • Veg Spring Rolls - Crispy rolls filled with fresh vegetables.
  • Chicken Tikka - Spiced and grilled chicken pieces.

Main Course

  • Butter Paneer Masala - Paneer cooked in creamy tomato and butter gravy.
  • Chicken Biryani - Fragrant basmati rice cooked with spiced chicken.
  • Dal Makhani - Slow-cooked black lentils with butter and cream.
  • Special Offer: Veg Thali - Roti, rice, dal, vegetable curry, salad and dessert.

Desserts

  • Gulab Jamun - Soft milk dumplings served with sugar syrup.
  • Chocolate Brownie - Warm chocolate brownie served with vanilla ice cream.

Beverages

  • Mango Lassi - Refreshing yogurt-based mango drink.
  • Masala Tea - Traditional Indian tea with aromatic spices.
  • Fresh Lime Soda - Chilled lime drink with soda.

Chef's Recommended Dining Experience

  1. Begin with our signature Paneer Tikka.
  2. Enjoy Chicken Biryani or Butter Paneer Masala as the main course.
  3. Refresh yourself with a Mango Lassi.
  4. Finish your meal with our Chocolate Brownie.
