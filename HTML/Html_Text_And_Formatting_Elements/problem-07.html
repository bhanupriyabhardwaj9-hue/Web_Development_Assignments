My Personal Blog


1. My Experience as a College Student

Starting college is a completely new experience compared to school. College provides students with more freedom, but it also comes with greater responsibility. Students have to manage their classes, assignments, practical work, examinations, and personal activities. Time management is one of the most important skills a college student can develop.

Apart from academics, college is also a great place to meet new people and develop communication and teamwork skills. Participating in ideathons, technical events, workshops, and group projects can provide practical experience and make college life more interesting. These activities can also help students become more confident.

2. My Journey in Programming

Programming is one of the skills I am interested in developing during my college journey. I have started learning Python because it has a simple syntax and is widely used in areas such as artificial intelligence, machine learning, automation, and data science. Regular practice is the key to becoming a better programmer.

I am also learning HTML to understand how websites are structured. Creating simple webpages has helped me understand headings, paragraphs, formatting elements, and the basic structure of a webpage. Every small program or webpage I create gives me an opportunity to learn something new.

In the future, I would like to learn more programming languages and technologies. I also want to work on personal projects so that I can apply what I learn in the classroom to real-world problems.

3. A Journey to Remember

Travelling is a great way to explore new places, cultures, food, and people. A trip can provide experiences that are very different from our normal daily routine. Visiting historical places and natural destinations can also help us learn more about the world around us. Travelling teaches us to be independent, adaptable, and open to new experiences.

One of the best parts of travelling is creating memories with friends and family. Exploring a new city, trying local food, and visiting famous landmarks can make a trip unforgettable. Every journey has something new to teach us.

I would like to travel to different parts of India and eventually visit other countries as well. My goal is to explore different cultures while experiencing the beauty and diversity of different places.

4. My Career Goals and Future Plans

My career goal is to become a successful engineer in the field of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I want to build a strong foundation in programming, mathematics, problem-solving, and modern technologies. My main goal is to develop skills that allow me to create useful solutions to real-world problems.

During my college years, I want to gain practical experience through projects, internships, ideathons, technical competitions, and other activities. These experiences can help me understand how technology is used outside the classroom. I believe that continuous learning and consistency are essential for achieving long-term career goals.

I also want to improve my English communication and teamwork skills. Technical knowledge is important, but being able to explain ideas, communicate with others, and work effectively in a team is equally valuable. My aim is to become a skilled, confident, and responsible professional.
