Annual College Fest 2026

Welcome to the Annual College Fest 2026! It is a three-day celebration of talent, creativity, sports, technology and entertainment. Students from different departments can participate in competitions, workshops and cultural events.

Fest Introduction

The Annual College Fest 2026 brings students together to showcase their skills and enjoy exciting competitions and cultural activities. The fest will be organized for three days and will include technical, cultural, literary and sports events.

All registered participants must carry their valid college ID card during the fest.

Registration Process

Students must complete the online registration before participating in any competition.

Registration Deadline: 5 October 2026

  1. Visit the official fest registration website.
  2. Select the competition in which you want to participate.
  3. Enter your name, college ID and contact details.
  4. Select Individual Event or Team Event.
  5. Pay the applicable registration fee.
  6. Submit the registration form.
  7. Save the registration/reference number for future use.

Rules

  • Participants must carry a valid college ID card.
  • Participants must report at the venue at least 30 minutes before their event.
  • Participants must follow the instructions given by coordinators.
  • Any form of cheating or unfair practice can result in disqualification.
  • Participants must maintain discipline and cleanliness on campus.
  • Damage to college property will not be tolerated.
  • Decisions of the judging panel will be considered final.

Important Definitions

Open Event
An event in which eligible students from different colleges can participate.
Team Event
An event in which two or more participants compete together as a team.
Individual Event
An event in which a participant competes individually.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling for a fest event.
Coordinator
The person responsible for managing and organizing a particular event.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition where selected participants compete for the final positions.
