Welcome to the Department Academic Portal. This portal provides students with important information about the weekly timetable, subjects, faculty members, academic rules and department activities.

Introduction

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering focuses on developing technical knowledge, programming skills and problem-solving abilities among students. Students participate in lectures, tutorials, laboratory sessions and practical activities throughout the week.

Important Notice: Students must check the timetable regularly for any changes in classrooms, faculty or practical sessions.

Sequence Before a Practical Class

  1. Check the practical timetable and laboratory number.
  2. Read the experiment instructions before entering the lab.
  3. Bring the required practical file and study material.
  4. Report to the laboratory on time.
  5. Listen to the faculty instructions carefully.
  6. Perform the experiment and record the observations.
  7. Submit the practical work for evaluation.

Academic Rules

  • Students must maintain at least 75% attendance.
  • Students must carry their college ID card.
  • Students should reach the classroom before the lecture begins.
  • Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode during classes.
  • Students must submit assignments before the given deadline.
  • Students must maintain discipline in laboratories.
  • Academic dishonesty is strictly prohibited.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A teaching session where the faculty explains theoretical concepts.
Lab
A laboratory session where students perform practical activities.
Tutorial
A smaller academic session focused on solving problems and clearing doubts.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation conducted during the semester through tests, assignments, attendance and other academic activities.
Practical
A hands-on academic activity designed to apply theoretical knowledge.
