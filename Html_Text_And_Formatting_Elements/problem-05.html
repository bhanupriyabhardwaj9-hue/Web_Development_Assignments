Science Notes

Chemistry

Water: H2O

Carbon Dioxide: CO2

Oxygen: O2

Sulfuric Acid: H2SO4

Ammonia: NH3

Atom: An atom is the smallest unit of an element that retains its chemical properties.

Physics

Area = Length × Breadth, measured in m2.

Volume = Length × Breadth × Height, measured in m3.

Speed = Distance / Time

Einstein's equation: E = mc2

Force = Mass × Acceleration (F = ma)

Force: Force is a push or pull that can change the motion of an object.

Biology

Cell: The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

DNA contains the genetic information required for the growth and development of living organisms.

Photosynthesis: 6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2

Chlorophyll is the green pigment that helps plants absorb light energy for photosynthesis.

Human body temperature is approximately 37°C.

Quick Revision

• Chemistry: H2O, CO2, O2, H2SO4, NH3

• Physics: F = ma, E = mc2, Area in m2, Volume in m3

• Biology: Cell is the basic unit of life and chlorophyll helps plants perform photosynthesis.
