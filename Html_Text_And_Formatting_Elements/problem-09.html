Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

1. Students must carry their valid college ID card while on campus.

2. Students must follow the instructions given by teachers and college authorities.

3. Students should maintain cleanliness in classrooms, laboratories, and other college areas. Keeping the campus clean is everyone's responsibility.

Attendance

1. Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in each subject.

2. Students should attend classes regularly and arrive on time.
Late entry may be restricted during ongoing lectures.

Important: Attendance below the required percentage may affect eligibility for examinations.

Examination

1. Students must carry their college ID card and examination admit card during examinations.

2. Use of mobile phones, electronic devices, or unauthorized study material during examinations is strictly prohibited.

Any student found using unfair means may face disciplinary action and cancellation of the examination.

Assignment Submission

1. All assignments must be submitted before the given deadline.

2. Assignments should be properly completed and submitted according to the instructions provided by the subject teacher.
Students should include their name, roll number, branch, and subject.

Deadline: All assignments must be submitted by 11:59 PM on the assigned due date.

Late submissions may receive reduced marks depending on the teacher's instructions.

Discipline

1. Students must behave respectfully towards teachers, staff, and fellow students.

2. Bullying, harassment, fighting, or damaging college property is strictly prohibited.

Students violating disciplinary rules may face penalties or further disciplinary action.

Important Notice

Students are expected to follow all college rules and regulations. Failure to follow mandatory rules may result in appropriate action.

These rules are intended to maintain a safe, respectful, and productive learning environment for all students.
