About University

Welcome to the University Information Portal. Our university is committed to providing students with quality education, practical knowledge, and opportunities for personal and professional development. The university encourages students to explore new ideas and develop skills that prepare them for successful careers.

The university provides a supportive academic environment where students can participate in classroom learning, projects, technical events, and innovation-based activities. Students are encouraged to develop both technical and communication skills.

Education, innovation, and skill development are important parts of the university's learning environment.

Departments

The university has several academic departments that offer education in different areas of engineering and technology. These include Computer Science and Engineering, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and Civil Engineering.

The Computer Science and Engineering Department focuses on programming, software development, databases, and computer systems. The Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Department focuses on modern technologies such as machine learning, data analysis, and artificial intelligence.

Students can select their field of study according to their interests and career goals. Practical learning and continuous improvement are important for success in every department.

Academic Programs

The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in various technical fields. The Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) program provides students with theoretical knowledge as well as practical experience.

Students studying engineering learn subjects related to mathematics, programming, science, and technology. For example, the area of a square can be represented as a2, while the volume of a cube can be represented as a3.

Scientific subjects may also include formulas such as H2O for water and CO2 for carbon dioxide. These concepts help students build a strong foundation for advanced technical subjects.

Regular attendance and active participation can help students understand their subjects more effectively. Students are also encouraged to participate in projects, ideathons, workshops, and other academic activities.
