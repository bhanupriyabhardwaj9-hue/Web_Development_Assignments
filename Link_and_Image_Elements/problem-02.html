Gaur Chowk Underpass Faces Delay as Traffic Troubles Continue in Greater Noida West

Ongoing construction has caused major congestion for daily commuters

Greater Noida West is facing increasing traffic problems as construction work on the Gaur City underpass continues to affect one of the area's busiest road corridors. The underpass is being developed to improve connectivity and reduce traffic pressure at Gaur Chowk, an important junction connecting Greater Noida with Noida, Ghaziabad and Delhi.

The construction has been ongoing for several months and has created long traffic queues, particularly during peak hours. Commuters travelling through the area have reported significant delays, with vehicles moving slowly through congested stretches. The ongoing construction has turned the Gaur City junction into a major traffic bottleneck for daily commuters.

The underpass is planned to be approximately 700 metres long and six lanes wide. Once completed, it is expected to allow vehicles to bypass the busy intersection and improve the movement of traffic between Greater Noida, Gaur City and the Delhi-Meerut Expressway.

The traffic situation has become particularly difficult during evening rush hours, when commuters returning from offices are joined by residents, commercial vehicles and shoppers travelling through the area. The construction has also caused dust and disruption around nearby roads. For many residents, reducing congestion and completing the project quickly has become an important priority.

The latest reports on 24 August 2026 indicate that the infrastructure work remains an important issue for commuters in the region. Authorities and residents are closely watching the progress of the project as they hope the completed underpass will improve traffic movement and connectivity.

Editor's Note

Infrastructure projects can cause temporary inconvenience, but timely completion and proper traffic management are important to reduce the impact on residents. Commuters are advised to follow traffic instructions and use alternative routes whenever possible.
