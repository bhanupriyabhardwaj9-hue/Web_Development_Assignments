GLA University Greater Noida

About

GLA University Greater Noida is an industry-integrated learning campus located in Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. The campus focuses on quality education, practical learning, industry exposure, and career development. Students are encouraged to gain knowledge through projects, internships, workshops, and real-world applications.

The university aims to prepare students for successful professional careers by combining academic learning with practical experience. Industry-focused education and skill development are important parts of the GLA Greater Noida learning environment.

Programs

GLA University Greater Noida offers various undergraduate and postgraduate programs. These include B.Tech CSE, B.Tech CSE with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, B.Tech Electronics and Computer Engineering, AI-enabled BBA, AI-enabled MBA, and MCA with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

The B.Tech programs focus on programming, computer science, artificial intelligence, machine learning, electronics, and other modern technologies. Students also get opportunities to work on projects and develop practical skills.

Campus Life

Campus life at GLA Greater Noida provides students with opportunities to participate in technical events, clubs, cultural activities, sports, workshops, projects, and other student activities. The university also focuses on industry exposure and early internships to help students gain practical experience.
