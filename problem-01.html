About The College

GLA University, Greater Noida, is a state private university established in the year 1991. GLA University is a co-educational institute which offers four degrees and 6 courses. It is recognised by several government councils. The university has been graded "A+" by NAAC
•GLA University, Greater Noida, Recognitions and Accreditations: The university is recognised by several councils like AICTE, PCI, BCI, UGC, AIU and has IACBE Accreditation.

•GLA University Greater Noida admissions 2026 are based on entrance exam score.

The university offers scholarships to the BTech students based on the marks obtained in PCM/ JEE-Mains Percentile. The quantum of fee waiver for the JEE Main category ranges from 25% to 90%. GLA University Greater Noida facilities offered include tech-enabled classrooms with projectors, specialised department libraries, and computer labs. The campus features 24/7 medical support (Aarogyam), varied sports facilities (basketball, football, indoor games), cafeterias, and hostels for students.
